Prior revision v0.12, HUG-G001-15 fix revision per Director disposition 2026-07-29 (accept/product): G001-15 F-01 → the two replay obligations are separated — HUG-SYS-006 binds ground-system replay to the ground system's native timestamped log recording and playback, with playback as the demonstration evidence, and states that the recorder-to-viewer replay package is a separate interface that does not discharge it. **Verification methods:** T = Test (executed checker), A = Analysis, I = Inspection, D = Demonstration (documented pass execution).

**Traceability rule:** every requirement traces up to a NEED-nn from the CONOPS and down to a checker, procedure, or campaign artifact. The matrix is generated by `tools/gen_traceability.py` from requirement IDs referenced in code and documents. Hand-maintained matrices are prohibited. Every T-verified requirement's checker must be demonstrated to fail when its condition is violated (negative check) before its passing result counts.

## HUG-EPS: Power model

| ID | Requirement | Ver. | Notes / trace |
|---|---|---|---|
| HUG-EPS-001 | Battery state of charge shall evolve per the energy balance of array generation minus load consumption, applying charge efficiency 0.95 and discharge efficiency 0.95. | T | NEED-02; HUG-SYS-001 |
| HUG-EPS-002 | Solar-array generation shall be zero in eclipse and shall ramp across eclipse transitions over no more than 10 s. | T | NEED-02; HUG-SYS-002 |
| HUG-EPS-003 | The EPS shall autonomously enter LOW_POWER mode when SoC falls below 30% and SAFE mode when SoC falls below 20%; each transition shall shed the payload load. Autonomous mode transitions shall be downward only; transition to a higher mode shall occur only by ground command (HUG-CMD-006). | T | NEED-03; CON-02; FMEA REC-01 |
| HUG-EPS-004 | The model shall support these injectable faults: (a) single array-string output forced to zero; (b) stuck-on load of 2.0 W; (c) housekeeping telemetry generation halt — from fault onset no housekeeping frames are generated or transmitted (observed effect: CDH_FRAME_CNT ceases advancing and no fresh values arrive), while spacecraft truth state continues to evolve; the fault latches until cleared by CMD_SYS_RESET, after which frame generation resumes with counters per HUG-CMD-005. | T | HUG-SYS-005; G001 F-03 |
| HUG-EPS-005 | EPS design envelope values (battery capacity 38 Wh, bus voltage 5.0 V, array peak generation 17 W, nominal bus load 4 W, payload load 3 W) shall be recorded in `defs/` and used consistently by model, dictionary limits, and FMEA. | I | NEED-04; Derive rule |

## HUG-CMD: Commanding

| ID | Requirement | Ver. | Notes / trace |
|---|---|---|---|
| HUG-CMD-001 | The spacecraft shall validate every received command and reject on: unknown opcode, out-of-range argument, or mode inhibit; each rejection shall increment the reject counter and set a reject-reason code in telemetry. | T | NEED-03 |
| HUG-CMD-002 | Payload activation (state = ON) shall be inhibited when EPS mode is SAFE or SoC is below 40%. | T | NEED-03; CON-04 |
| HUG-CMD-003 | Payload sampling-rate commands shall accept only 0.1-10 Hz and shall be rejected when the payload is OFF. | T | NEED-02 |
| HUG-CMD-004 | SAFE-mode entry commanded from the ground shall always be accepted regardless of mode. | T | NEED-03 |
| HUG-CMD-005 | System reset shall clear the stale-telemetry fault state, reinitialize command counters to zero and then apply its own acceptance increment — so the first post-reset housekeeping frame reads CDH_CMD_ACC_CNT = 1 and CDH_CMD_REJ_CNT = 0, which constitutes the reset acknowledgement — and preserve battery SoC. | T | NEED-03; G001 F-04 |
| HUG-CMD-006 | The mode-set command shall command EPS mode to NOMINAL or LOW_POWER only; it shall be rejected (ARG_RANGE) for any other target, and rejected (MODE_INHIBIT) when EPS_BATT_SOC is below 35% for target NOMINAL or below 25% for target LOW_POWER. A commanded transition to LOW_POWER shall shed the payload load; a transition to NOMINAL shall not re-enable the payload. | T | NEED-03; CON-02; FMEA REC-01 |
| HUG-CMD-007 | Command accounting shall be exactly-once: each received command shall increment exactly one of CDH_CMD_ACC_CNT or CDH_CMD_REJ_CNT by exactly one, and the increment plus any commanded telemetry effect shall be visible no later than the next housekeeping frame (≤ 1 s at the nominal rate). CMD_SYS_RESET is the sole exception to simple delta arithmetic; its acknowledgement is the post-reset counter epoch per HUG-CMD-005. Absence of any counter delta within 10 s of a verified send is the loss-of-commandability declaration threshold used by PROC-003's command-path branch. | T | NEED-03; G001 F-05 |

## HUG-OPS: Operations

| ID | Requirement | Ver. | Notes / trace |
|---|---|---|---|
| HUG-OPS-001 | Written procedures shall exist for: nominal activation pass (PROC-001), low-battery response (PROC-002), loss-of-telemetry-or-command response (PROC-003). Each procedure step shall carry a stable step identifier (PROC-NNN-Snn); each step shall name the role executing it and the telemetry it checks; and each decision step shall name the observation that selects each of its branches. | I | NEED-02; FMEA REC-02; G001-07 F-01 |
| HUG-OPS-002 | Every executed pass shall produce a pass report containing: pass plan, event timeline, limit violations, actions with procedure step references, a per-step completion record, and disposition of anomalies. The per-step completion record shall contain exactly one entry for every step of the governing procedure, keyed by the step's stable identifier (HUG-OPS-001), carrying: a completion state from the bounded set {completed, not applicable, omitted}; for a not-applicable entry, the stated basis — the condition or branch outcome that made the step inapplicable; for a completed decision step, the recorded observation or decision outcome that selected the branch taken; for an omitted decision step, the record that no observation was made and no branch was selected; and for an omitted entry, a reference to its anomaly disposition. Post-pass review shall compare the completion record against the governing procedure; an applicable step without a completed or omitted entry, or a step without any entry, is an omission finding. An omission finding shall be recorded and dispositioned as an anomaly in the pass report's disposition-of-anomalies content, retained truthfully as an omitted entry, with corrective action defined: re-execution of the missed step at the next opportunity where feasible — recorded as a completed entry for the re-executed step and referenced from the omitted entry — or an explicit owner risk acceptance — a documented acceptance with rationale approved by the design authority (the owner), recorded in the pass report and referenced from the omitted entry. The omitted state is truthful and permanent for the pass in which the step was missed. | I | NEED-04; G001-06 F-01; G001-07 F-01; G001-07 F-02; G001-08 F-01; G001-09 F-01; G001-10 F-01 |

| HUG-OPS-004 | Each anomaly pass shall additionally produce an anomaly report with cause, response evaluation, and any resulting change requests to procedures or requirements. | I | NEED-04 |
